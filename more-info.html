Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a prescription to order?

Prescription (Rx) medicines require a valid prescription uploaded through our system. A licensed pharmacist will verify it before your order is prepared. Over-the-counter (OTC) medicines can be purchased without a prescription.

How does Click & Collect work?

Browse medicines online, add them to your cart, and choose your preferred branch — either Punturin or Malinta. You'll receive a notification via SMS or email once your order is ready for pick-up. Simply visit the branch and present your order reference.

How long does order preparation take?

Most orders are prepared within the same business day if placed before noon. Orders placed after noon may be ready for the following business day. You will receive a notification once your order is ready.

How are prescriptions verified?

Once you upload a photo or PDF of your prescription, our system extracts the details automatically. A licensed pharmacist then reviews the prescription for dosage accuracy, drug interactions, and authenticity before approving your order.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept GCash, Maya, bank transfer, and debit/credit card payments. Cash payment is also accepted in-store upon pick-up.

Can I get medicine delivered to my home?

Currently, our system supports in-store pick-up (Click & Collect) only. Home delivery may be introduced in future updates. Stay tuned for announcements.

What if I need a refill reminder?

You can set up refill reminders through your customer account. We'll send you an SMS or email notification when it's time to restock your regular medicines.
