About Angel's Pharmacy

Serving the Valenzuela City community with safe, accessible, and professional pharmaceutical care.

Who We Are

Angel's Pharmacy is a community-focused pharmacy serving residents of Valenzuela City across our Punturin and Malinta branches. We are committed to making medication access safer, more convenient, and more transparent through our web-based pharmacy information system.

Our platform allows patients to browse real-time medicine availability, upload prescriptions for pharmacist verification, and reserve orders for in-store pickup — reducing wait times and improving overall service quality.

Licensing & Compliance

Angel's Pharmacy operates in full compliance with Philippine healthcare safety standards and is licensed under DOH pharmacy guidelines.

All prescription medicines require pharmacist approval before being dispensed. We do not dispense controlled substances without proper documentation.
